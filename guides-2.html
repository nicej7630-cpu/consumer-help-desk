Guides

Short, practical guides to help you understand your rights and act on a problem.

Getting a refund

  • Contact the seller in writing (email is best), and describe the problem clearly.
  • Keep receipts, order numbers, screenshots and any messages.
  • Give a reasonable deadline for a response, such as 14 days.
  • If you paid by card, you can also ask your card provider about a chargeback.

Faulty goods

  • Goods should be of satisfactory quality, fit for their purpose, and as described.
  • Report a fault as soon as you notice it, and keep the item and its packaging.
  • Your rights can differ by country and by the type of purchase, so check your local rules.

Online shopping

  • Check the seller's name, address and contact details before you buy.
  • Read the return policy and delivery times.
  • Pay with a card or a secure method that offers protection.

Subscriptions

  • Read the cancellation terms before you sign up, and note any free-trial end date.
  • Cancel in writing, and keep proof of the date you cancelled.
  • If charges continue after cancelling, contact your bank or card provider.

Money and banking

  • Contact your bank or card provider straight away if you see a payment you don't recognise.
  • Your bank will never ask for your password or a one-time code by phone or email.
  • Ask about a chargeback or dispute, and keep a record of every call and message.
