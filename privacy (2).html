Privacy notice

This notice explains what personal information the Consumer Help Desk collects when you use this website, why we collect it, and what choices you have. We've written it in plain language. If anything is unclear, please email info@consumerhelp.live.

Please note: we will never ask for your banking passwords, one-time codes or full card numbers. Please don't send them to us.

Who we are

The Consumer Help Desk is an independent information service. We are not a government agency, regulator or court. The website is consumerhelp.live.

What we collect

If you file a complaint
Your name, email address, the name of the business, a description of the problem, the date it happened, the amount involved (if you give it), how you paid, and any order or reference number you choose to include.
If you share your experience
The name or initials you choose to give (optional), your country (optional), and the story you write. We only store it after you tick the consent box.
If you email us
Your email address and whatever you write in the message.

Why we collect it

  • To review your complaint, and to contact you about it.
  • To publish a shared story on this website, but only after we have reviewed it and you have agreed.
  • To answer your emails and keep a record of our replies.

Who can see your information

  • Complaints are visible only to the Help Desk administrator. No one else can read them.
  • Shared stories are published only after approval, and only with the name or initials and country you chose to give.
  • We do not sell your information, and we do not share it with advertisers.
  • When a complaint is sent, a notification with its details is passed to Web3Forms, a form-to-email service, so that the Help Desk can be alerted. It is used only for this purpose.
  • Our website and data are hosted with service providers (GitHub for the website, and Google Firebase for the private database). They process information only to run this service.

How long we keep it

We keep a complaint while we are working on it, and for 24 months after it is closed. After that, it is deleted.

Shared stories are kept while they are published on this website. You can ask us to remove a story at any time, and we will remove it.

Feedback answers on guides are kept without any personal details, to help us improve the guides.

Cookies and third-party tools

This website doesn't use tracking or advertising cookies. It uses two services from Google: fonts for the typeface, and the translation tool at the top of each page. When you use the translation tool, the text of the page is sent to Google to be translated, as described in Google's own privacy policy.

Your choices

  • You can ask to see the information we hold about you.
  • You can ask us to correct it, or to delete it.
  • You can ask us to remove a shared story at any time, even after it has been published.
  • You can withdraw your consent at any time, without giving a reason.

To make any of these requests, email info@consumerhelp.live from the address you used on the form, so we can confirm it's you.

Changes to this notice

If we change this notice, we will update the date below and, where the change is important, tell people who have contacted us.
