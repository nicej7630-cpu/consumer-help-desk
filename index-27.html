Scam alerts

Fake delivery messages

Texts asking you to pay a small fee to receive a parcel. Don't click the link; check with the courier directly.

Too-good-to-be-true offers

Very cheap goods from unknown sellers, often with no reviews. Pay by a method that offers protection.

Urgent bank requests

Calls or emails asking for your password or code. Your bank will never ask for these.

Guides

Getting a refund

Start by contacting the seller in writing, keep receipts and screenshots, and give a clear deadline.

Faulty goods

Goods must be of satisfactory quality and as described. Report faults promptly and keep the item.

Online shopping

Check the seller's contact details, read the return policy, and pay with a card or secure method.

Subscriptions

Look for the cancellation terms before you sign up, and keep proof of any cancellation request.
