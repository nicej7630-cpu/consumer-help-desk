We're here to help you understand your options. We don't take payments, and we'll never ask for your banking details.

When you email us, please include:

  • The name of the business or seller
  • What happened, and the date
  • The amount involved and how you paid
  • Any order numbers or screenshots (don't send passwords or full card numbers)
